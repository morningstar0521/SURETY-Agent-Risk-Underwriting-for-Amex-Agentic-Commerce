SURETY CERTIFIED AGENT PROGRAM
Business Brief - CodeStreet 2026


OVERVIEW

SURETY Certified is a trust-tier program for AI agent developers building on the
Amex ACE Developer Kit. Every registered agent already carries a continuous risk
score. The program makes that score visible and rewards developers who keep it
low, turning compliance from an obligation into a competitive advantage.


TIER STRUCTURE

Tier      Risk score (0-100)   Standing
Gold      0 - 30               Lowest risk. Widest exposure limits.
Silver    31 - 65              Moderate risk. Standard limits.
Bronze    66 - 100             Elevated risk. Tight caps, entry level.

Tiers are recalculated continuously from live behaviour: intent deviation,
amount anomaly, merchant entropy, category consistency and velocity.


QUALIFICATION

An agent must hold its tier for 90 consecutive days to be certified at that
level. A single sustained breach resets the clock. Certification is a record of
behaviour over time, not a one-off assessment.


BENEFITS

Gold      Highest default per-transaction and per-window exposure limits.
          Reduced authentication friction at checkout. A visible "SURETY
          Certified" badge displayed to Card Members at the point of purchase.

Silver    Moderate default limits. Standard authentication. No badge.

Bronze    Entry-level limits with tight per-transaction caps. Agents begin here
          and earn their way up.


VALUE FOR DEVELOPERS

Higher tiers mean fewer declined transactions and fewer step-up authentication
prompts, which means faster checkouts and less abandonment. The badge is a
public signal of reliability that competing agents cannot claim without earning
it. Good governance stops being a cost of doing business and becomes a reason
Card Members choose one agent over another.


VALUE FOR AMERICAN EXPRESS

Recurring certification fees create a new revenue line attached to the agent
developer ecosystem rather than to transaction volume alone. Tighter caps on
lower-tier agents reduce fraud and dispute losses, making Agent Purchase
Protection actuarially sustainable. And the program compounds: as more
developers seek certification to reduce friction, Amex sees more agent
behaviour, which improves the underwriting model, which makes the tiers more
valuable to hold. The closed-loop network makes this defensible - an issuer or
a processor alone cannot observe both sides of the transaction.
